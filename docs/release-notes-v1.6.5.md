# Kitty Army v1.6.5

Kitty Army brings two pieces of pink-and-gold magic to Minecraft Bedrock:
summon a squad of 20 custom tuxedo kitties with the Kitty Whistle, or equip the
Kitty Transformation Collar and become the same kitty while retaining normal
player controls.

## Highlights

- Twenty owner-specific kitties arrive in four sparkling waves.
- Army kitties follow their summoner and fight nearby monsters.
- A second whistle sends each kitty along its own outward native-navigation
  route before pink-particle cleanup.
- The collar performs a quick dark compression and sparkle transformation.
- Common third-person held items, armor, capes, and elytra are hidden while the
  kitty form is active; normal presentation returns after removing the collar.
- No experimental world toggles are required on Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.45+.

## Install

Download `Kitty Army.mcaddon`, open it with Minecraft Bedrock, and enable the
Kitty Army Behavior Pack. Its linked Resource Pack should activate
automatically. Fully leave and reopen an existing world after updating.

Kitty Army is an unofficial Minecraft add-on and is not approved by or
associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
